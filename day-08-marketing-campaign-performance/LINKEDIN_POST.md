# LinkedIn draft — Day 8

📣 **Day 8 of my 30 Days, 30 Projects challenge: Marketing Campaign Performance Analytics**

Today I built a campaign reporting workflow to compare channel and campaign performance across the marketing funnel.

What I created:
• Generated six months of synthetic daily performance data for 12 campaigns across six channels
• Used SQL to aggregate campaigns, channels, and monthly trends
• Calculated CTR, conversion rate, CPA, and ROAS from the funnel metrics
• Built visual summaries for channel return, monthly spend and revenue, and campaign efficiency

📊 **On this simulated dataset:**
• Portfolio ROAS: **8.54x**
• Portfolio CTR: **4.65%**; click-to-conversion rate: **5.01%**
• Email had the highest channel ROAS at **20.88x**; Video had the lowest at **1.25x**
• Search produced the most revenue in aggregate, at about **2.98 million synthetic currency units**

One takeaway: campaign dashboards are useful for comparing observed performance, but ROAS alone doesn’t tell us whether a channel caused incremental sales or whether more budget would improve results.

All records, costs, conversions, and revenue are synthetic. These findings demonstrate the analysis workflow and are not real campaign benchmarks or budget recommendations.
